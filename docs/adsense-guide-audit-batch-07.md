## Batch conclusion

- **10 content routes source-screened.**
- **Highest-priority fact checks:** FreshBooks/Wave exact prices and eligibility; HubSpot/Pipedrive and HubSpot/Zoho free-plan limits; HR/payroll country coverage; inventory feature and region availability.
- **Original-value opportunities:** the two software-selection articles should include distinct, reusable worksheets and realistic worked examples rather than repeating generic advice.
- **Overlap watch:** the two software-selection guides need different purposes—portfolio/tool-count decisions versus a structured vendor pilot.
- These are source-screening classifications, not a guarantee of AdSense suitability or factual accuracy. No live-page rendering, link-status verification, vendor checkout, product testing or external benchmark verification was performed.

## Review method and guardrails

Each source file was inspected for page intent, unique decision support, workflow specificity, pricing/plan assertions, external source links, regional claims and overlap. Numeric prices, quotas, country availability and legal/HR capabilities require separate current primary-source verification before publication. Do not invent hands-on testing, first-hand experience, customer outcomes or credentials.
