## Batch conclusion

- **10 content routes source-screened.**
- **Critical content-quality findings:** three workflow articles (client projects, freelancer leads and previously identified freelancer invoicing) use generic comparison/template content that does not adequately fulfil their own search intent. These deserve priority over cosmetic edits.
- **Highest-priority fact checks:** Mailchimp/Brevo/MailerLite quotas, multi-currency accounting tiers and fees, and developer API/model terms.
- **Original-value opportunity:** n8n workflow blueprints can become a distinctive practical resource if versioned, tested and labelled honestly.
- These are source-screening classifications, not a guarantee of AdSense suitability or factual accuracy. No live-page rendering, link-status verification, vendor checkout, product testing or external benchmark verification was performed.

## Review method and guardrails

Each source file was inspected for page intent, practical usefulness, workflow specificity, pricing/plan assertions, external source links, regional claims and overlap. Numeric prices, quotas, model/API terms, licence conditions and country availability require separate current primary-source verification before publication. Do not invent hands-on testing, first-hand experience, customer outcomes or credentials.
