## Batch conclusion

- **9 content routes source-screened; 1 redirect alias excluded.**
- **Highest-priority content improvements:** FreshBooks alternatives and free-vs-paid business software.
- **Overlap cluster:** no-credit-card pages for CRM, invoicing and project management need a narrow, genuinely verified sign-up focus rather than repeating their parent comparison pages.
- **Regional risk:** invoicing and payment-processing products have market-specific eligibility and fees. Do not imply that US products or payment methods work for every international reader.
- These are source-screening classifications, not a guarantee of AdSense suitability or factual accuracy. No live-page rendering, link-status verification, vendor checkout, product testing or external benchmark verification was performed.

## Review method and guardrails

Each source file was inspected for page intent, unique decision support, practical workflow guidance, pricing/plan assertions, external source links, regional claims and overlap. Numeric prices, quotas, country availability and card requirements need separate current primary-source verification before publication. Do not invent hands-on testing, first-hand experience, customer outcomes or credentials.
