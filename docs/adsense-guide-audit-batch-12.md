## Batch conclusion

- **11 content routes source-screened; 1 redirect alias excluded.**
- **Highest-priority content fix:** Trello alternatives needs product-specific comparison and switching guidance rather than interchangeable template rows.
- **Highest-priority fact checks:** time-tracking prices/usage fees, Trello/Asana free and paid plan gates, Zoho/Wave eligibility and fees, hosting renewal terms.
- **Regional caveat:** the QuickBooks/Wave/Zoho pricing examples in this catalogue are often US-specific. Wave's stated business-country eligibility is especially important; do not recommend it to a Sri Lankan business as if it were locally available.
- These are source-screening classifications, not a guarantee of AdSense suitability or factual accuracy. No live-page rendering, link-status verification, vendor checkout, product testing or external benchmark verification was performed.

## Review method and guardrails

Each source file was inspected for page intent, unique decision support, workflow specificity, pricing/plan assertions, external source links, regional claims and overlap. Numeric prices, quotas, monitoring features, platform availability, payment fees and hosting performance require separate current primary-source verification before publication. Do not invent hands-on testing, first-hand experience, customer outcomes or credentials.
