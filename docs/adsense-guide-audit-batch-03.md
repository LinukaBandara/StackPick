## Review method and guardrails

Each source file was inspected for page intent, unique decision support, practical workflows, pricing/plan assertions, external source links, dated claims and overlap with nearby guides. Current plan details and country availability require separate primary-source verification. Do not invent hands-on testing, first-hand experience, customer outcomes or credentials.
