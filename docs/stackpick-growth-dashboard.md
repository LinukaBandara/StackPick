## Available evidence

The supplied Search Console Coverage CSV reports impressions only for 2026-10-08 through 2026-10-12: 0, 0, 5, 17, and 90 respectively (the first two rows have blank indexing counts). It contains no clicks, queries, page breakdown, country, device, or revenue. These few days are a snapshot, not a stable baseline or forecast.

No Analytics, AdSense, or affiliate reporting was available. Revenue, RPM, conversion rate, commissions, and the $1,000/day aspiration cannot be modeled from this evidence without inventing inputs.

## Measurement plan

After confirming the configured Analytics property and consent behavior, record 28-day and 90-day Search Console clicks/impressions/CTR by query and landing page, country, device, indexing state, and sitemap processing. Record analytics sessions only for consented traffic. Add outbound affiliate events only when links are active and measurement is compliant. Add ad impressions/RPM and partner conversions only after those systems are approved and reporting is available.

Keep the period, source, and extraction date with every baseline. Compare like periods and annotate substantial content changes. Treat small samples cautiously.
