## Proposed first article

Working title: **Best Software for Tutoring Businesses: Solo Tutors vs Small Tutoring Centers**

Possible URL: /best/tutoring-business-software

Search-intent hypothesis: a buyer is trying to choose software to run a tutoring operation, not merely host video lessons or sell an online course.

Cover two distinct reader profiles:

- **Independent tutor:** recurring one-to-one lessons, a simple calendar, student notes, payment tracking, reminders, and minimal admin.
- **Small tutoring center:** multiple tutors, room/calendar conflicts, attendance, parent communication, lesson packages, invoicing, payroll or tutor payouts, and reporting.

Comparison criteria:
- recurring and rescheduled lessons
- multi-tutor calendars and conflict handling
- attendance and lesson notes
- parent/student portal and communications
- billing model: per lesson, lesson packs, recurring subscriptions, or invoices
- payment processing and transaction fees
- tutor payroll or payout workflow
- free-plan limits, trial requirements, and number of students/tutors
- export and cancellation path
- total cost for a solo tutor versus a small center

Potential products to verify from official sources include TutorBird, TutorCruncher, Teachworks, Oases Online, Tutorbase, and general-purpose alternatives only where they genuinely fit the workflow. Do not rank a tool first because its marketing page says so. Do not present vendor-published feature claims as independent hands-on test results.
