## Verification rules

- Never treat an official public affiliate page as proof that StackPick has applied, been accepted, or received a payment.
- Before applying, check country eligibility, payout rail availability in Sri Lanka, identity/tax paperwork, minimum payout, payment currency and fees.
- Record the official source URL, date checked, current program terms and application confirmation.
- Before activating links, add a clear affiliate disclosure near recommendations, use appropriate sponsored-link attributes, test tracking, and keep editorial recommendations independent.
- A high advertised commission is not evidence of likely conversion or revenue. Prioritize products that genuinely fit StackPick's audience and existing editorial coverage.
