# StackPick SEO Keyword Strategy — Q3/Q4 2026

Research window: approximately May–October 2026. Primary target markets: United States, United Kingdom, and Australia. Business goal: grow qualified organic search traffic and earn AdSense revenue; affiliate monetization can be evaluated separately.

## Research notes and limitations

- Search-volume and CPC estimates below are third-party published snapshots, not live Google Search Console data for StackPick.
- Google Ads CPC is a signal of advertiser competition/value, **not an AdSense RPM forecast**. AdSense revenue depends on the visitors StackPick actually earns, ad impressions, geography, page experience, and auction outcomes.
- Before finalizing a publishing calendar, validate the clusters in Google Ads Keyword Planner with country set separately to United States, United Kingdom, and Australia, and review Google Trends over the last 3–6 months. Keyword Planner supports location, date-range, average monthly searches, and bid-range filtering: https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6325025
- Google Trends values are normalized relative-interest scores, not absolute search volumes: https://support.google.com/trends/answer/4365533

## Recommended priority order

### Tier 1 — publish, strengthen, and internally link first

1. **Accounting software for small business**
   - Primary intent: best accounting software for small business
   - Supporting cluster: small business bookkeeping software; accounting software pricing; Xero vs QuickBooks; QuickBooks alternatives; accounting software for freelancers
   - Country variants should only be published when the page contains real regional comparisons and verified pricing/features.

2. **Payroll software for small business**
   - Primary intent: best payroll software for small business
   - Supporting cluster: payroll software pricing; payroll software for small teams; Gusto alternatives; QuickBooks Payroll vs Gusto
   - Local facts: US payroll/tax form support; UK PAYE; Australian STP. Do not make unsupported compliance claims.

3. **Help desk / customer support software**
   - Primary intent: best help desk software for small business
   - Supporting cluster: help desk software pricing; ticketing software for small teams; Help Scout vs Freshdesk; Zoho Desk alternatives; free help desk software
   - Compare ticket limits, seats, automation limits, channels, and upgrade triggers.

4. **Inventory management software**
   - Primary intent: best inventory management software for small business
   - Supporting cluster: inventory software pricing; inventory software for retail; inventory software for ecommerce; Zoho Inventory alternatives
   - Compare SKU/order limits, integrations, multi-location support, and add-on costs.

### Tier 2 — maintain and build comparison clusters

- CRM: best CRM for small business; free CRM limits; HubSpot vs Pipedrive; CRM alternatives.
- Invoicing: invoicing software for freelancers; invoice software pricing; Wave alternatives; FreshBooks vs QuickBooks.
- Project management: project management software for small teams; ClickUp vs Asana; free plan limits.
- Time tracking: time tracking software for freelancers; Clockify vs Toggl; billable-hours tracking.
- Expense management and POS: focus on business size, regional availability, integrations, and total plan costs.

## Page template requirements

Every priority comparison should answer the query quickly and provide original decision-making value:

1. A clear verdict and who the recommendation fits.
2. A dated pricing/plan table, with currency and region labelled.
3. Free-plan and paid-plan limits, seat/SKU/transaction caps, and common add-on costs.
4. Pros, cons, meaningful limitations, and the best reason not to choose each tool.
5. A small-business scenario or workflow example that is specific to the category.
6. A transparent methodology, editorial update date, and links to official pricing/product documentation.
7. A useful FAQ based on genuine query intent, not repetitive keyword stuffing.
8. Internal links to related comparisons and head-to-head pages.

## Country strategy without doorway pages

Do not create three near-identical US/UK/Australia pages just to swap country names. Keep a shared comparison page when the products/features are materially the same, and include clearly labelled regional sections only where supported. Create a dedicated regional page only when its pricing, product availability, integrations, tax/payroll workflow, or shortlist differs enough to provide substantial unique value.

## Measurement loop

1. In Keyword Planner, run the same seed list separately for US, UK and Australia; export monthly searches, competition, and top-of-page bid ranges.
2. In Google Trends, compare the topic/terms over the most recent 3–6 months for each country.
3. In Search Console, track impressions, clicks, CTR, average position, query, page, and country.
4. Refresh content based on actual impressions and query gaps every 2–4 weeks; don't rewrite pages only because a third-party tool predicts a high CPC.
5. After AdSense approval, monitor page RPM and country-level performance. Do not equate advertiser CPC with expected publisher revenue.
