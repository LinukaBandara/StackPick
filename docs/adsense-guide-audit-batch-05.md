## Batch conclusion

- **10 content routes source-screened.**
- **Strong/useful foundations:** several pages have clear practical intent, but they still need current plan, legal, regional or privacy verification.
- **Highest-priority fact checks:** expense-management pricing/eligibility, free accounting regional terms, form-builder limits, CRM free-plan quotas and shift-scheduling claims.
- **Cross-site consistency risk:** CRM limits recur across multiple guides; maintain one verified fact record or source register so identical vendor claims do not drift between pages.
- **Regional scope risk:** several guides target US pricing or eligibility while StackPick has an international audience. Label US-specific comparisons clearly and avoid implying those products or plans are available in every market.
- These are source-screening classifications, not a guarantee of AdSense suitability or factual accuracy. No live-page rendering, link-status verification, vendor checkout, product testing or external benchmark verification was performed.

## Review method and guardrails

Each source file was inspected for page intent, distinct decision support, workflow usefulness, pricing/plan assertions, external source links, date claims, legal/privacy statements and overlap. Numeric prices, quotas, country availability and legal capabilities require separate current primary-source verification before publication. Do not invent hands-on testing, first-hand experience, customer outcomes or credentials.
