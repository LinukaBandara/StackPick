## Evidence details and source URLs

### 1. Invoice clients as a freelancer
- FreshBooks: Lite lists 5 billable clients, Plus 50 and Premium unlimited. Its page currently displays promotional offers alongside standard list prices; do not present the promotional price as the standard price. https://www.freshbooks.com/pricing
- Wave: its payment fee and payout documentation distinguishes US and Canadian processing availability and timing. https://www.waveapps.com/pricing and https://support.waveapps.com/hc/en-us/articles/218323823-Online-payments-processing-fees-and-timelines
- Zoho Invoice: the linked free plan page is specifically for Indian businesses and lists 2 users, 3 projects and 500 invoices per year. https://www.zoho.com/in/invoice/pricing/

### 2. Manage customer leads as a freelancer
- HubSpot Free: up to 2 users and 1,000 contacts; free access has no time limit. Other standard object records have separate limits. https://www.hubspot.com/pricing/crm
- Pipedrive: four subscription plans (Lite, Growth, Premium, Ultimate) plus optional add-ons and top-ups. https://support.pipedrive.com/en/article/how-does-pricing-work-in-pipedrive
- Trello Free: up to 10 collaborators and 10 boards per Workspace, plus 250 Workspace command runs per month. https://trello.com/pricing

### 3. Free vs paid business software
- HubSpot: 2-user and 1,000-contact Free limits. https://www.hubspot.com/pricing/crm
- Trello: 10 collaborators, 10 boards and 250 Workspace command runs per month. https://trello.com/pricing
- Notion: 5 MB per file upload, 7-day page history, and limited collaborative blocks for workspaces with 2 or more members on Free. https://www.notion.com/pricing
- ClickUp: Free Forever includes 60 MB storage, unlimited tasks and unlimited free members, with view/form limits. https://clickup.com/pricing

### 4. Manage client projects without a full-time team
- ClickUp: 60 MB Free Forever storage and unlimited tasks/members; its view limits page specifies restrictions on forms and Gantt uses. https://clickup.com/pricing and https://help.clickup.com/hc/en-us/articles/32274881672599-Views-feature-availability-and-limits
- Trello: 10 boards and 10 collaborators per Workspace, plus 250 monthly command runs. https://trello.com/pricing
- Notion: 5 MB file uploads and 7-day page history on Free; collaborative block limits apply for 2+ members. https://www.notion.com/pricing

### 5. AI tools for small-business marketing
- Canva: Free includes up to 20 uses of Standard AI tools or Premium AI tools per month; allowance varies by tool category. https://www.canva.com/help/ai-access-variantb/ and https://www.canva.com/pricing/
- Adobe Express: Free lists daily free generations; Premium lists 250 generative credits per month. https://www.adobe.com/express/pricing

### 6. Calendly vs Google Calendar
- Calendly Free: one event type and one calendar connection. https://calendly.com/pricing
- Google Calendar: a personal Google Account or Workspace Business Starter can create one booking page; some advanced appointment-schedule features require eligible Google Workspace or Google One subscriptions. https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/16287038

### 7. Notion vs ClickUp
- Notion Free: 5 MB file uploads, 7-day page history and limited blocks for 2+ members. https://www.notion.com/pricing
- ClickUp Free Forever: 60 MB storage, unlimited tasks and unlimited free members; certain views/forms have usage limits. https://clickup.com/pricing and https://help.clickup.com/hc/en-us/articles/32274881672599-Views-feature-availability-and-limits

### 8. Canva vs Adobe Express
- Canva Free: 5 GB storage and up to 20 Standard/Premium AI uses per month; Pro lists 100 GB storage and a larger AI allowance. https://www.canva.com/pricing/ and https://www.canva.com/help/ai-access-variantb/
- Adobe Express: Free lists daily free generations; Premium lists 250 generative credits per month. https://www.adobe.com/express/pricing

## Editorial safeguards

- Do not claim hands-on testing. These are current official-source checks only.
- Do not state a promotional price as the normal recurring price.
- Do not generalize region-specific availability or pricing. The Zoho Invoice source above is India-specific; Wave payment processing is documented for the US and Canada.
- Keep a checked date beside changing plan facts and recheck them before a material article update.
- Keep the final recommendation tied to user workflow, not just a plan feature count.
