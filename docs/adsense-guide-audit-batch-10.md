## Batch conclusion

- **9 content routes source-screened; 1 redirect alias excluded.**
- **Highest-priority content fix:** Notion alternatives currently has generic, interchangeable product descriptions and needs a genuine product-specific decision matrix.
- **Highest-priority factual checks:** form-builder prices/quotas, password-manager security/seat terms, payroll costs and coverage, CRM prices and POS processing/contract terms.
- **Regional scope:** payroll and POS comparisons are explicitly US-oriented; avoid presenting them as globally available recommendations.
- These are source-screening classifications, not a guarantee of AdSense suitability or factual accuracy. No live-page rendering, link-status verification, vendor checkout, product testing or external benchmark verification was performed.

## Review method and guardrails

Each source file was inspected for page intent, unique decision support, workflow specificity, pricing/plan assertions, external source links, regional claims and overlap. Numeric prices, quotas, security capabilities, contract terms and country availability require separate current primary-source verification before publication. Do not invent hands-on testing, first-hand experience, customer outcomes or credentials.
